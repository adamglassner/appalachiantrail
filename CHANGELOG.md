# Changelog

All notable changes to The Appalachian Trail, an Ironite game.

## v1.3.0 — October 20, 2026

### Added
- New painted Appalachian title-screen wallpaper:
  - Layered Blue Ridge ridgelines at golden hour, with autumn forests and valley mist
  - A thru-hiker on a rocky overlook beside a wind-bent pitch pine, with spruces and a white blaze framing the scene
- The wallpaper is built in layers that shift slightly with the mouse for depth, with mist drifting slowly through the valleys
- The wallpaper is also included as a 2560×1440 desktop image
- Windows installer (`AppalachianTrail-Setup-1.3.0.exe`):
  - Installs just for you, with no administrator rights needed
  - Adds Desktop and Start menu shortcuts
  - Adds an uninstall entry in Settings → Apps
  - Updates in place, keeping your saves
- New app icon (Blue Ridge ridges at sunset with a white blaze), plus Ironite version details on the .exe
- Portable .zip download for playing without installing

### Changed
- The title, setup, credits and other title-menu screens now use the painted wallpaper instead of the 3D map overview

## v1.2.0 — October 20, 2026

### Added
- Credits screen on the title menu, crediting Ironite
- "What's New" screen on the title menu that shows this changelog in-game
- Ironite logo on the startup and loading screens
- Version number on the title screen
- Godot Engine license notice in the credits

## v1.1.0 — October 18, 2026

### Added
- Original soundtrack in an old-time and bluegrass style, with guitar, banjo, fiddle, harmonica and bass
  - "Springer Dawn" plays on the title screen
  - "Ridge Walk" plays on the trail
  - "Trail Town" plays in towns
  - "Shelter Fire" plays at night
  - There is also a foraging tune, a victory piece and a game-over piece
- Nature and weather ambience that follows the weather and the time of day: forest birdsong, night crickets with a campfire and an owl, rain, thunderstorms, mountain wind, rivers and town bustle
- Sound effects:
  - Footsteps while hiking
  - Thunder that follows each lightning flash
  - Bear growls, river splashes and snake rattles
  - Event chimes, a cash register and a camera shutter
  - Foraging pickups, a bear whistle and button clicks
- Sound Settings with Master, Music, Nature & weather and Effects volume sliders. The game remembers your settings.

### Changed
- In the foraging minigame, Space now blows a bear whistle instead of shouting

## v1.0.0 — October 18, 2026

### Added
- First release: an Oregon Trail-style thru-hike from Springer Mountain, Georgia to Mount Katahdin, Maine (2,198 miles)
- Party of 1–4 named hikers, each with health, morale and hunger, who earn trail names along the way
- Three professions with different budgets and score multipliers, and four start dates
- Daily choices for pace (Slow, Steady or Push) and rations (Full, Half or Bare), plus rest days
- Supplies: food, water capacity, stove fuel, first-aid kits, spare shoes and gear condition
- 44 real landmarks and trail towns, each with a general store, hostel, diner, outfitter and other hikers to talk to
- Random events: bears, river crossings, lightning on exposed ridges, illness and injuries, trail magic, getting lost in fog and more
- Landmark challenges:
  - The Smokies permit
  - The half-gallon ice cream challenge
  - Mount Washington's weather
  - Mahoosuc Notch
  - The Kennebec ferry
  - The 100-Mile Wilderness
- Foraging minigame with in-season wild food, poisonous mushrooms and bears
- Stylized 3D overworld built from the trail's real coordinates:
  - Forests that change with the seasons
  - A day/night cycle
  - Rain, snow, fog and lightning
- 3D diorama scenes for events
- Journal, autosave and continue, a Hall of Fame and the October 15 Katahdin deadline
