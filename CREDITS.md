## Soundtrack
All music is composed and produced by Ironite.

- "Springer Dawn" (title screen)
- "Ridge Walk" (on the trail)
- "Trail Town" (in towns)
- "Shelter Fire" (night)
- "Wild Harvest" (foraging)
- "Baxter Peak" (victory)
- "The Long Way Home" (game over)

## Special thanks
To every thru-hiker, section hiker, trail angel, ridgerunner, hostel owner and volunteer trail crew who keeps the white blazes leading north.

## Inspired by
The classic educational trail games of the 1980s and '90s: The Oregon Trail, The Amazon Trail, The Yukon Trail and African Trail.
